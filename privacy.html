Privacy Policy

Erpliwave · Last updated: 16 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Erpliwave ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your Zoom account to Erpliwave and use our online-class scheduling features. By connecting your Zoom account, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

Erpliwave is an education-management platform used by coaching institutes and their teachers to schedule and run online classes. Our Zoom integration lets a teacher or institute connect their own Zoom account so that class meetings can be created automatically on their behalf.

2. Information we access from Zoom

When you connect your Zoom account, we access only what is required to operate the integration:

  • Basic Zoom profile (user:read) — your Zoom user ID, account ID, and email address, used to identify the connected account and link it to your Erpliwave profile.
  • Meeting management (meeting:write) — to create and configure Zoom meetings for the classes you schedule in Erpliwave.
  • OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens issued by Zoom, stored securely so we can create meetings on your behalf without asking you to log in each time.

We do not access meeting recordings, chat messages, contacts, or any data beyond the scopes listed above.

3. How we use the information

  • To create, schedule, and provide join links for online classes you set up.
  • To display your connection status and the Zoom account associated with it.
  • To maintain a valid connection by refreshing OAuth tokens.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. How we store and protect data

Data is stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with access restricted by row-level security and server-side service roles. OAuth tokens are stored on our backend and are never exposed to other institutes, teachers, or students. All traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

5. Data sharing

We do not share your Zoom data with third parties, except infrastructure providers (such as our database and hosting providers) that process data solely on our behalf to run the service.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain your Zoom connection and tokens only while your account remains connected. You can disconnect your Zoom account at any time from within Erpliwave, which deletes the stored tokens and connection record. You may also revoke access directly from your Zoom account under Settings → Installed Apps. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact us using the details below.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
