Erpliwave Live Classes — Zoom Integration Guide

Last updated: 16 June 2026

Erpliwave Live Classes lets a teacher or institute connect their own Zoom account so that Zoom meetings for online classes are created automatically when a class is scheduled. This guide explains how to add, use, and remove the integration.

1. Adding the app (connecting Zoom)

  1. Log in to Erpliwave at your institute's URL with your teacher or institute-admin account.
  2. In the left sidebar, open Live Classes.
  3. Click the Connect button in the top-right of the page.
  4. You are redirected to Zoom. Review the requested permissions and click Allow to authorize.
  5. You are returned to Erpliwave and the status changes to Connected, showing your Zoom email.
Permissions requested: user:read (read your basic Zoom profile to identify your account) and meeting:write (create Zoom meetings for your classes). The app only ever acts on your own Zoom account.

Troubleshooting adding the app

  • "You cannot authorize this app" — your Zoom account admin must allow the app, or you must be signed in to the correct Zoom account. Sign in with the Zoom account you want to use for classes.
  • Redirected back but not connected — the authorization link may have expired. Click Connect again to restart.
  • Still stuck? Contact us at support@erpli.com.

2. Usage

Feature: Connect a Zoom account

Use case: Link your Zoom account once so classes can be hosted on it.

Prerequisites: A Zoom account, and a teacher or institute-admin role in Erpliwave.

Feature: Create a live class (auto-create Zoom meeting)

Use case: Schedule an online class; a Zoom meeting is created automatically on your connected account.

Prerequisites: Your Zoom account must be connected (see section 1).

  1. On the Live Classes page, click Create Live Class.
  2. Enter the class title, start time, and the batch(es) of students.
  3. Save. A Zoom meeting is created on your Zoom account and a join link is stored.
  4. Enrolled students see the class in their student app and join via the meeting link at the scheduled time.

Feature: Join / open a class

Use case: Open the class to start or join the Zoom meeting.

Prerequisites: A scheduled class with a Zoom meeting.

3. Removing the app (disconnecting Zoom)

Option A — Disconnect from inside Erpliwave

  1. Open Live Classes.
  2. Click Disconnect next to your connected Zoom status.
  3. Confirm. Your stored Zoom OAuth tokens are deleted immediately.

Option B — Remove from your Zoom account

  1. Sign in to the Zoom App Marketplace at marketplace.zoom.us.
  2. Go to Manage → Added Apps (or Settings → Installed Apps).
  3. Find Erpliwave Live Classes and click Remove.
Implications of de-authorization: Once you disconnect or remove the app, Erpliwave can no longer create Zoom meetings on your behalf, and any future classes will need a new connection or a manually pasted meeting link. Data removal: we delete your stored Zoom OAuth tokens and connection record. Meetings already created on your Zoom account remain in your Zoom account and are managed there. To request deletion of any remaining associated data, email support@erpli.com.
